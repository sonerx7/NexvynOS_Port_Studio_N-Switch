# Age of Empires II native launcher preview for Nexvyn OS

- Age of Empires II was created by Ensemble Studios and originally published by Microsoft.
- The Nexvyn OS launcher, DRS/DAT validator, and RTS experience profile are distributed under GPL-3.0. The corresponding source and build instructions are in the Nexvyn Port Studio repository under `GamePorts/aoe2`.
- Dear ImGui is included under the MIT license. See [LICENSE-IMGUI.txt](LICENSE-IMGUI.txt).
- Upstream decompilation tracking targets `https://github.com/samuelcaldas/age-of-empires-2-decomp`. PC target decompilation is currently at 0% matching progress, and executable splits require proprietary IDA Pro database exports. Full engine execution is pending.
- The base bundle contains no original maps, textures, graphics, audio, or game data. Users supply their own legitimate Age of Empires II installation to validate data files.
