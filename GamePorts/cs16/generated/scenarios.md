# Acceptance scenarios: fps

Run each in every declared input mode, in handheld (720p, touch) and in docked (1080p, no touch screen). Record pass/fail, the console mode, and where it ran (host, emulator, physical Switch).

## Walk, aim and fire (`move_aim_fire`)

- [ ] gamepad: Left stick, right stick, ZR
- [ ] touch: Left on-screen stick, drag right side, Fire button
- [ ] kbm: W A S D, mouse, left click

## Open the buy menu, choose a weapon, confirm, close (`buy_menu`)

- [ ] gamepad: Open with its button, D-pad to move, A to buy, B to close
- [ ] touch: Open with the on-screen button, tap the weapon, tap close
- [ ] kbm: Open with B, press the number, Esc to close

## Switch weapon (`weapon_switch`)

- [ ] gamepad: Bumpers or Y
- [ ] touch: Tap the weapon icon
- [ ] kbm: Number keys or wheel

## Open the pause menu and resume (`menu`)

- [ ] gamepad: + then B
- [ ] touch: Menu button then back
- [ ] kbm: Esc twice
